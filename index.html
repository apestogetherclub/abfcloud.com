abfcloud

Private services for one family.

What this is

abfcloud runs a small set of self-hosted services for the members of one family. It isn't a public service: there is no sign-up, and accounts are created by invitation only.

Email we send

We send account email only, and only to members who have an account:

  • password resets you ask for
  • email address verification
  • account recovery codes

We never send marketing or newsletters, and we never send to purchased or shared lists. Addresses that bounce or report a message as spam are stopped automatically.

Privacy

For each member we keep a name, an email address and sign-in details, and use them only to run the services. We don't sell or share them, and we don't track visitors to this page.

Contact

Questions, or an email from us you didn't expect? Write to hello@abfcloud.com.
